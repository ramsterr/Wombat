01 Our story

A roastery, not a factory.

Two roasters.
One drum.
No shortcuts.

Ember started in 2019 with a one-kilo sample roaster in a garage on SE Division and a stubborn idea: coffee should taste like the place it grew, not the machine that roasted it.

We still buy small — whole lots, named farms, one harvest at a time. We roast twice a week, twenty kilos a batch, and cup every roast before it goes into a bag. If it isn’t right, it doesn’t ship. That’s the whole business plan.

We’d rather sell out than stretch a batch.
Maren Okafor & Ellis Reyes Founders, Ember Coffee Roasters

03 How we roast

Slow is the only shortcut.

We roast on Tuesdays and Fridays. Everything ships within a week of the drum — as fresh as coffee gets before it needs to rest.

  1. 01

    Source

    Whole lots, bought direct or through importers we’ve known for years. Named farms, one harvest at a time — never a blend by accident.

    Year-round
  2. 02

    Sample

    Every offer is cupped three times, blind, before we commit. Most don’t make it. The ones that do get a name and a place on the shelf.

    3 cups
  3. 03

    Roast

    Twenty kilos, one profile, logged every thirty seconds. We adjust by ear and by smell as much as by the readout on the drum.

    11–13 min
  4. 04

    Rest & ship

    Four days in a one-way valve bag. CO₂ out, oxygen out, and the fruit comes forward. Then it ships, within 48 hours of your order.

    4 days
